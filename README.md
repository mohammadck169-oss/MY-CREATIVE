# Mohammed Nishad CK — AI Content Creator Portfolio

An editorial portfolio website built for **Mohammed Nishad CK** (`vavachan_ai`), an AI Content Creator and Video Editor.

The design is directly adapted from the custom Canva presentation deck aesthetic, featuring an ivory and olive-green palette, luxury serif typography, floating tilted pill badges, an interactive Instagram frame mockup, slide deck mode, and case studies for featured projects.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Features

- **Canva Editorial Aesthetic**: Warm ivory linen background (`#FAF9F5`) with deep olive green typography (`#445133`), rounded pill borders, and editorial italic headlines.
- **Editorial Cover Slide**: Bold minimalist Black & White typography statement (`I AM AI CONTENT CREATOR`).
- **Floating Tilted Badges**: Modeled after Slide 2 (`WHO —> I AM`) with floating pills tilted at playful angles.
- **Dual Viewing Modes**:
  - **Web View**: Smooth-scrolling high-end portfolio layout.
  - **Slide Deck Mode**: Presentation mode that lets visitors navigate slide-by-slide with arrow keys (`←`, `→`, `Space`, `Esc`) or bottom controller.
- **Obsidian Night Mode**: Dark theme toggle featuring obsidian olive styling.
- **Case Study Modals**: Full-screen deep dives for:
  - **TOM**: AI Cinematic Animation Reel
  - **DALLA & LUQMA**: Commercial Brand Campaign & Meta Ads
  - **CARTOON**: Original AI Character IP & Storytelling
  - **PERFUME**: Luxury Product Visualization
- **Instant WhatsApp Inquiry Engine**: Form that formats project details and opens WhatsApp directly.

## 🚀 Running Locally

To preview locally:
1. Simply double-click `index.html` to open in any web browser, or
2. Run a local server:
   ```powershell
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\server.ps1
   ```
   and navigate to `http://localhost:5500/`.
